# 🗳️ A Topology of Voting Behaviour in the 2024 UK General Election

Traditional regression models can tell us *if* something matters.  
But Topological Data Analysis (TDA) tells us *how it behaves across space and people*.

In my dissertation project for my MSc Data Science dissertation at The University of Manchester, I used **Topological Data Analysis (TDA)** to unravel the complex, interconnected forces behind voting behaviour in the **2024 UK General Election**. From migration and education to age and employment, I examine how these socioeconomic variables shape constituencies — not linearly, but in clusters, pockets, and outliers. Visualised through point clouds, the project shows geographical heterogeneity of Labour-winning areas and homogeneity in Conservative strongholds.


📌 *This approach contributes to emerging research applying data science to political and electoral analysis. I discuss policy implications and recommendations based on findings.*

---

## 🧰 Methods and Tools

- **TDABM (BallMapper)**: A Topological Data Analysis tool used to visualise clusters of constituencies based on multiple variables.
- **OLS Regression**: For benchmarking and statistical comparison.
- **Python**: Used for data processing and visualisation.
- **Excel**: Used for initial data cleaning and preprocessing.
- **Census 2021 Data** (England & Wales only)
- **UK General Election 2024 Results**

---

## 🧠 Key Research Questions

1. How do socioeconomic variables predict constituency-level voting patterns in the UK?
2. To what extent do post-Brexit migration patterns impact the political landscape?
3. Can demographic changes predict shifts in voting behaviour?
