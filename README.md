# 🗳️ A Topology of Voting Behaviour in the 2024 UK General Election

This repository contains my research project and supplementary files for my MSc Data Science dissertation at The University of Manchester. The study investigates how voters' socioeconomic, migration, and demographic profles jointly influence voting behaviour at constituency-level in the 2024 UK General Election, with a focus on visualising high-dimensional data using **Topological Data Analysis BallMapper (TDABM)**. Visualised through point clouds, the project shows geographical heterogeneity of Labour-winning areas and homogeneity in Conservative strongholds.  

## 📄 Abstract

Traditional statistical methods often struggle to capture the complex, non-linear, and geographically nuanced factors driving voting behaviour. This study employs **TDABM**, in combination with **OLS regression**, to map multidimensional Census and voting data and uncover patterns missed by conventional techniques. 

Key findings suggest that:
- Labour-voting constituencies exhibit higher heterogeneity in socioeconomic and demographic characteristics than Conservative strongholds.
- Migration, employment, education, and household demographics significantly influence voting patterns.
- TDABM enables novel visualisation of joint distributions and constituency clusters, offering valuable political insight.

📌 *This approach contributes to emerging research applying data science to political and electoral analysis.*

## 🧠 Key Research Questions

1. How do socioeconomic variables predict constituency-level voting patterns in the UK?
2. To what extent do post-Brexit migration patterns impact the political landscape?
3. Can demographic changes predict shifts in voting behaviour?
